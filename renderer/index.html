Stabilization

Mark the shaky clips in Resolve, find them here and stabilize. PostPilot picks fixed details itself and works in a copy of your timeline.

Tick every timeline that has shaky clips; they run one after another.

A point marker selects its whole clip. Multiple markers on the same clip select it once. The top visible video clip is selected at each marker. Existing Fusion effects are kept for manual review.

After the run: ▶ Review jumps Resolve to the clip in the stabilized copy, ⇄ Original to the same clip in your timeline. ✔ Approve copies the stabilization onto your clip (your edit, grade and audio stay as they are) and gives it back its own colour; ↺ Undo takes it off again.

PostPilot saves the project and works in a timeline copy. For each clip it reads 5 frames to pick details, tracks about 8 of them in one Fusion pass (classic point trackers) and uses only the movement they agree on. Lock (still camera): the picture is pinned to the background. Smooth (operator reframes): slow camera moves are kept and only the fast shake is removed. The smallest zoom that hides black edges is added (max 1.03). Green = Passed. Orange = needs a look. No frame-by-frame export.

        Encoder

        Convert any video with your Resolve presets, built-in or custom, like Media Encoder. Each group has one preset and output folder; any file can use its own instead. Drop files onto a group to add them there, or anywhere else on this page to start a new group. Each file keeps its own frame rate and size; temporary items in Resolve are removed afterwards.

        after min s

        Encodes in a separate empty temporary Resolve project. After your confirmation, PostPilot automatically saves and closes your open project. When encoding finishes, it removes the temporary project and your saved project stays closed, ready for your selected after-queue action. No open project is required. Keep the temporary project open while encoding. The queue is saved automatically after every change.
